# Two-factor authentication (2FA)
## Turn on 2FA
Open Settings, then Security, then Two-factor authentication. Scan the QR code with an authenticator app such as Google Authenticator or Authy and enter the 6-digit code.
## Backup codes
When you turn on 2FA we show 10 one-time backup codes. Save them somewhere safe. Each code works once.
## Lost your device?
Sign in with a backup code. If you have no backup codes, contact support. Identity verification takes 2 to 3 business days.
