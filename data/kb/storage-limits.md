# Storage and file size limits
## Plans
Free includes 5 GB of storage, Plus includes 200 GB, and Pro includes 2 TB.
## Maximum file size
A single file can be up to 2 GB on Free, 10 GB on Plus, and 50 GB on Pro.
## When you run out of space
Uploads pause when your storage is full. Delete files, empty the trash, or upgrade your plan to continue.
