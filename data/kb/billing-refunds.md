# Billing and refunds
## Refund policy
Annual plans can be refunded within 14 days of purchase. Monthly plans are not refundable, but you can cancel at any time and keep access until the end of the billing period.
## How to request a refund
Go to Billing, then Request refund, and choose the charge. Approved refunds return to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.
## Invoices
Invoices are emailed after every payment and can be downloaded from Billing, then Invoice history.
