# How to reset your password
## Reset from the login page
1. Go to the NimbusDrive login page and click "Forgot password".
2. Enter the email address linked to your account.
3. Open the email titled "Reset your password" and click the link within 30 minutes.
4. Choose a new password with at least 12 characters.
## Not receiving the email?
Check your spam folder. Reset emails are sent from no-reply@nimbusdrive.example. If nothing arrives after 10 minutes, contact support.
